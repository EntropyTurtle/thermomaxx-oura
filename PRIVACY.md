# Privacy Policy — Thermomaxx Oura Integration

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

Thermomaxx is a personal, noncommercial home automation project.

This application connects my Oura account to my privately operated Home Assistant system for sleep, wellness, and home automation purposes.

- **Data collected:** Oura health and wellness information authorized through the Oura API, including sleep, readiness, activity, and physiological measurements.
- **Data usage:** Information is used for personal dashboards, analysis, and home automation.
- **Data storage:** Data retrieved by the integration may be stored locally within my Home Assistant system and its backups. Oura separately processes data under its own privacy policy.
- **Data sharing:** The application does not intentionally sell or share Oura data with advertisers or other third parties.
- **Data deletion:** Locally stored information can be removed by deleting the integration and associated data and backups.
- **Access:** The application is intended solely for my personal use and is not offered as a public service.

For privacy inquiries, use the contact email associated with the application's Oura developer registration.
